# 🗣️ Announcements

Staying connected with the community is the heartbeat of any masjid, and we wanted to make reaching your congregation as easy as sending a text. Whether it’s a change in the *Iqamah* time, asking someone to move their car, or an urgent community update, the Announcements tool has you covered.

### What’s included?

- **Title:** A quick headline
- **Body:** You’ve got a 500-character limit to get your message across (If you find yourself hitting that limit often, just let us know and we can look into bumping it up!)
- **Images (Optional):** You can attach a photo or graphic to make the post pop
- **Priority Levels:** Mark it as urgent if it’s something the community needs to see immediately

Once you hit send, it goes straight to the phones of everyone following your masjid. Check out the scribe below to see exactly how to blast out your first update!

# 🗓️ Events

Events are what bring communities together. Without them, we wouldn't have a dedicated space to share knowledge, grow spiritually, or just hang out with friends. Whether it’s a Nueces Night, Tasty Tuesday, or a guest speaker, the Events feature helps you get the word out and get people through the doors.

### What’s included?

- **Title:** The name of your event
- **Description:** Tell the community what to expect. You’ve got plenty of space here to share the "who, what, and why."
- **Image (Required):** Every event needs a flyer or a nice header image
- **Date & Time:** Let everyone know exactly when to show up so they can clear their schedules.
- **Location:** Whether it’s in the main musalla, the courtyard, or somewhere on campus, make sure people know where to find you

Events stay visible until they’ve passed, making it easy for users to plan their week around your masjid’s schedule. Check out the scribe below to see how to create your first event!

# 🕋 Prayer Times

Prayer times are the heartbeat of the masjid. No mosque app would be complete without them, which is why we’ve focused on making the prayer time experience as accurate and flexible as possible for your community.

### What’s included?

- **Automatic & Manual Times:** Whether your masjid follows a specific calculation method or you want to set custom "static" Iqamah times for the month, you’ve got total control
- **Monthly Prayer Chart:** A quick, one-page reference for the entire month’s timings
- **Jummah Times:** Easily list multiple Khutbah times so your community knows exactly when to show up for Friday prayer
- **Calculation Methods:** Choose from standard methods (like ISNA, MWL, etc.) to ensure the Adhan times align perfectly
- **Location Based:** Everything is synced to your masjid’s specific coordinates for pinpoint accuracy

Check out the scribe below to see how to update your masjid’s prayer times!
